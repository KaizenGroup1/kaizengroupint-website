About

Kaizen is the practice of small, continuous improvement — the idea that steady progress beats dramatic effort. It is the principle our products are built around, and the reason we care more about whether something is still being used in six months than whether it impressed anyone in the first week.

Kaizen Group International LLC is a Maryland limited liability company, registered on 18 August 2022 and existing under the laws of the State of Maryland, United States. We are a diversified innovation and solutions company built on the philosophy of continuous improvement — creating smarter businesses, digital products, services and systems that solve real-world problems, improve how people work and live, and get better with every iteration.
