Kaizen Family — Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

Kaizen Family holds the ordinary details of running a household — who is where, what needs doing, what is for dinner. We treat that as private by default. This policy sets out exactly what is stored, the few things that are sent outside our systems and why, and how to get rid of any of it.

The short version.

Your household's information is stored in the United States and is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. We do not build advertising profiles and there are no advertisements in the app.

When you ask Kaizen to read a notice or suggest meals, that request goes to Anthropic's Claude — but only the specific thing you asked about. Your calendar, your tasks, your shopping list and your family's names are never sent.

You can delete anything you have sent in, and you can delete your account and your household's data outright, from inside the app.

Who we are

Kaizen Family is published by Kaizen Group International LLC, of 6200 Westchester Park Drive, #704, College Park, MD 20740, United States. For anything in this policy, write to privacy@kaizengroupint.com.

What we store

Your account

  • Your email address, and a display name if you give one.
  • Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a cryptographic hash. Nobody at Kaizen Group can read it.

Your household

  • The household name, its time zone, and its currency.
  • Each member's name and role — owner, adult, child, or caregiver — and a birthday if one is entered.

What you put in the app

  • Calendar events: title, notes, location, times, and who they involve.
  • Tasks and chores: title, notes, due dates, who they are for, and completions.
  • Meals: names, ingredients, preparation time, and which day they are planned for.
  • Shopping list items: name, quantity, category, and price if you record one.
  • Food preferences: allergies, dietary restrictions, dislikes, favourite meals, and how long you have to cook on a weeknight.
  • Your weekly grocery budget, if you set one.
  • Anything you send to the Family Inbox — the text you paste, and any photograph or screenshot you upload.

Usage records

  • For each request to the assistant, we record what kind it was, when, which model answered, how many tokens it used, what it cost and whether it succeeded. We deliberately do not store the text of the request or the answer in these records.
  • Your notification preferences, which are per person rather than per household.
  • Your subscription status, if you subscribe.

Where it is stored

In a hosted PostgreSQL database and file storage provided by Supabase, in the United States (AWS us-east-1). Photographs you upload go to a private storage bucket — there is no public link to any of them, and viewing one requires a short-lived signed URL generated for that request.

If you use the app from outside the United States, your information is transferred there. By using Kaizen Family you consent to that transfer.

What is sent to the AI assistant, and what is not

Kaizen's assistant is built on Claude, from Anthropic. Every request goes through our own server first, which decides what to include. We send the minimum the request needs to be answered correctly.

When you ask for What we send
A notice to be read The text you pasted, or the photograph you uploaded, plus today's date and your household's time zone so relative dates like "next Friday" can be worked out.
Meal suggestions Your recorded allergies, dietary restrictions, dislikes, favourite meals and weeknight preparation limit, plus today's date. Allergies are sent so they can be avoided.

What is never sent: your name or your family members' names, your email address, your account identifier, your calendar, your tasks, your shopping list, your address, or your household's name.

Anthropic processes these requests on our behalf. Under our commercial terms with them, your content is not used to train their models.

Nothing the assistant suggests is saved until you accept it. Suggestions are shown to you and only become an entry in your calendar or your list when you tap to add them. That is enforced in our own architecture, not left to careful handling: the server component that talks to Claude has no ability to write to your calendar or task list at all.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your own device. Kaizen Family does not register a push token with any server, and the contents of a reminder never leave your phone. If you switch reminders off, nothing is scheduled.

Children

Kaizen Family is for adults running a household. It is not directed to children, and children do not have accounts — there is no way for a child to sign in.

An adult may add a child as a household member, which stores that child's name, their role, and a birthday if the adult chooses to enter one. That is the only information about a child the app holds, and it is entered by a parent or guardian for their own household's use. Removing a member, or deleting the household, removes it.

We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to privacy@kaizengroupint.com and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Delete anything you sent to the Inbox. Open it under "Sent in before" and delete it. This is a real deletion, not a hidden flag — the note, anything the assistant found in it, and any photograph are removed. Entries you already accepted stay in your calendar or list, because they are yours now.
  • Delete individual records — any event, task, meal or shopping item — from the app.
  • Delete your account. Settings, at the bottom. This removes your account and your household's data.
  • Ask us to stop analytics for your account. There is no in-app switch for this today, and we would rather say so than describe a control that does not exist. Write to privacy@kaizengroupint.com and we will exclude your account.
  • Turn off notifications in the app's notification settings, or in your phone's settings for Kaizen.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, export or restrict the processing of your information. Write to privacy@kaizengroupint.com and we will respond within the time your local law requires.

How long we keep it

Your household's information is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed. Usage records that contain no household content — which kind of request, when, what it cost — may be retained for billing and abuse prevention.

Security

Access is enforced in the database itself rather than only in the app: rules attached to every table restrict each row to the household it belongs to, so one family's information cannot be returned to another's account. Uploaded photographs sit in a private bucket reachable only through short-lived signed links. Our AI credentials are held server-side and are never shipped inside the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If we discover a breach affecting your information we will tell you and any regulator we are required to notify.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for anything material we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
